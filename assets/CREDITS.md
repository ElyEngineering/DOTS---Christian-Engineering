# Photography and fonts

## Boundary Waters regional photography

- `northwoods-hero.webp`: U.S. Forest Service, via the U.S. Department of the Interior. [Original photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/usinterior/52648180422/). Public domain.
- `ely-panorama.webp`: U.S. Forest Service, via the U.S. Department of the Interior. [Original photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/usinterior/52648947589/). Public domain.

These photographs depict the Boundary Waters region of northern Minnesota. They are not photographs of the community's property. Images have been resized and compressed for the web.

## Photographs from the original Ely website

- `levi.webp`: Levi Levinson's portrait, reused with the owner's permission from [the original About page](https://www.ely.technology/pages/about).
- `loons.webp`: Photograph of two loons, from [the original About page](https://www.ely.technology/pages/about). The photograph's precise location is not independently established.
