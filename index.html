01 / The vision

A good life is something we build together.

Build with purpose.
Love with your life.

Our most important work begins with the people entrusted to us.

Ely Christian Engineers brings together men who want to become faithful husbands, build strong Christ-centered families, and do excellent work. The vision is a national Christian community with a real home in Ely, Minnesota: a place where marriage, family, friendship, and vocation belong to the same life.

We write code, design systems, and explore what artificial intelligence makes possible. We also want our homes to reflect Christ’s love for the church. Technical ability and a flourishing career can serve a family and sustain a community. They make the most sense when we know what we are building for.

01

Sacrificial love

Christ “gave himself up” for the church. We seek that same generosity toward our wives, fiancées, and future marriages: love expressed through attention, faithfulness, patience, and the willingness to put another’s good before our own convenience.

02

Servant leadership

Initiative, provision, protection, and humility before God. We want to grow into men our families can depend on, ready to listen, carry responsibility, and serve. The character of our leadership should be recognizable in the way we care for the people closest to us.

03

Integrity in engineering

Honest work. Carefully built systems. Truthful communication. The character we bring to marriage should also shape our research, our code, and our commitments to one another. Craftsmanship matters because the work will be used by real people.

03 / Life in Ely

Northern Minnesota. A different kind of possibility.

Room to think.
Room to put down roots.

There is a reason this vision is taking shape here. A life close to the water and woods can make room for focused work, shared meals, unhurried conversations, and time with the people who matter most.

The Ely property now encompasses 51 acres, with the addition of a third property and a third cabin. That physical setting is part of the invitation: build useful things while making a home in a small-town community close to lakes and forest.

The goal is more than somewhere to open a laptop. It is the possibility of neighbors who know one another, families who spend time together, and a setting where a demanding technical life can coexist with attention to home. Housing arrangements, visits, and participation are conversations to have personally with Levi.

Engineering in service of a life worth building.

Serious minds.
Lasting purpose.

AI research, engineering, and profitable work can help families thrive and give this community a durable foundation.

01

Learn with intention

Bring curiosity, the willingness to study, and the discipline to develop a skill well. The original Ely vision joined study, work, and software development. That spirit continues in an invitation to grow alongside people who care about both the quality of their work and the lives it supports.

02

Build things that matter

AI is opening new questions for researchers and new possibilities for builders. We are interested in useful systems, sound engineering, and opportunities to turn technical ability into real value. There is room in the conversation for experienced engineers and for serious learners developing their craft.

03

Make work support home

Careers, ventures, and productive work are part of the larger purpose: supporting marriages, raising families, and sustaining a community. Talk with Levi about your experience, your interests, and what might fit. Specific roles, compensation, study arrangements, and availability are discussed individually.

05 / An invitation beyond Ely

Come for the vision.
Bring your whole life.

For husbands, future husbands, engineers, and builders who want their faith to shape the way they love and work.

This is a family-centered vision. Wives and families are central to the life we hope to build together. Whether you are already married, preparing for marriage, or thinking seriously about the kind of man you want to become, the invitation begins with a shared desire for faithfulness.

You do not have to live in northern Minnesota to start a conversation. The ambition is national, with Ely as an anchor. Some may be exploring a move; others may be looking for friendship, collaboration, or a way to contribute from where they are.

Conservatives, people on the center-right, and those who think of themselves as “JFK Democrats” are welcome in the conversation. Shared Christian commitments and a constructive interest in family, community, and useful work give us common ground.
