faithful homes.

Strong families.

We’re calling Christian men from across the country to consider a life in Ely: stronger marriages, good families, and serious work at the frontier of AI.

The heart of this mission is becoming better husbands and building families shaped by Christ’s love. Ephesians 5:21–33 gives us the pattern: reverence for Christ, sacrificial love, and faithful care for one another.

AI research is part of that shared life. Whether you already work in the field or want to develop a career in it, we want to help you explore that path while putting down roots and growing in faithfulness at home.
